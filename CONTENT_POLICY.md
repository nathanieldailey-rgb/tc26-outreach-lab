# Content and Rights Policy

This policy keeps the prototype useful for public outreach without turning the repository into a substitute for the journals. It applies to editorial prose, bibliographic records, scripts, storyboards, images, audio, video, and other non-code material.

## Separate licenses

The repository's software source code is offered under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

Editorial and media rights are separate. Existing project-original editorial material is **all rights reserved pending a committee decision** on a durable public-content license. The MIT code license does not grant permission to reuse that content.

## Material that may enter the repository

- Factual bibliographic metadata and canonical DOI links.
- Original outreach prose and short navigation descriptions created for this project.
- Committee-approved material for which the contributor documents the exact source version and the applicable license or permission.
- Original diagrams, scripts, storyboards, captions, and transcripts with documented authorship.

Every editorial pull request must identify the source version, DOI where applicable, license or permission, author, and any AI assistance. “Open access” is not enough by itself; record the specific license and the version to which it applies.

## Material that must stay out

- Publisher abstracts, publisher text, full-text articles, full papers, PDFs, copied tables, or copied figures unless written permission and the exact reuse terms have been reviewed and recorded.
- Any subscription corpus, harvested journal collection, embeddings derived from restricted text, or publisher scraping.
- Publisher, society, or committee logos and marks until their owners document permission for this specific use.
- Secrets, private hostnames, personal filesystem paths, private correspondence, reviewer comments, or personal data not intended for publication.
- AI-generated claims presented as official committee conclusions.

Link readers to the DOI landing page to read or purchase the full paper. Original articles should interpret and orient; they should not reconstruct the source publication.

## Contribution grant

By submitting editorial or media content for inclusion, the contributor represents that they own or control the necessary rights and grants the project maintainers a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to reproduce, display, distribute, format, and adapt that contribution in this repository, its public demonstrations, and successor versions of this outreach project. The contributor retains copyright and attribution. Third-party material is covered only to the extent of the separately documented license or permission.

If you cannot make that contribution grant, open an issue describing the rights constraint before submitting the content. A link or citation can often replace copied material.

## AI-assisted work

Disclose material AI assistance in the issue or pull request. The named human contributor remains responsible for checking every claim, source, quotation, right, and accessibility artifact. Do not provide subscription material or confidential information to an AI service. Ask responses and generated drafts are not official statements and are not endorsed simply because they appear in this prototype.

## Review and takedown

Maintainers may edit, decline, unpublish, or replace content when evidence, rights, safety, accessibility, or committee-status concerns arise. Report a rights concern through the repository's issue path with enough detail to identify the item; do not post private documents or credentials.
